Justifying the State Edited by Gene Callahan & Leye Komolafe

Co-editor

Gene Callahan

New York University

Gene Callahan has a PhD in political theory from Cardiff University and an MS in philosophy from the London School of Economics. He is the author of the books Economics for Real People and Oakeshott on Rome and America. He has also edited the volumes Tradition Versus Rationalism, Critics of Enlightenment Rationalism, Critics of Enlightenment Rationalism Revisited, and The Concept of Work in the History of European Philosophy. His book Oakeshott and Voegelin on the Nature of Political Philosophy is forthcoming from Palgrave.
