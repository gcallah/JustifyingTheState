Contributor

Jeff Carroll

West Virginia University

Jeff Carroll is an assistant professor at West Virginia University in the John Chambers College of Business and Economics in the department of General Business. He is a faculty member in the Kendrick Center for an Ethical Economy. His research is on creative destruction, economic growth, and how to theorize about justice. He has published papers in The Journal of Politics, Journal of Business Ethics, and Utilitas, amongst others.
