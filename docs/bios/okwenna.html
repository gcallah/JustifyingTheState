Contributor

Chrysogonus Okwenna

University of British Columbia

Chrysogonus Okwenna is a PhD student in Philosophy at the University of British Columbia. He completed his Master’s degree in Philosophy at Simon Fraser University, where he was a Darcy and Karina Biagioni Graduate Scholar. His research interests centre on moral philosophy, with a particular focus on biomedical ethics and applied ethics. He is also deeply engaged in social epistemology, African philosophy, socio-political philosophy, and moral psychology. Chrys is the founder of the Ijiroro Network for Young African Scholars and the founding editor-in-chief of Ijiroro: African Students’ Journal of Philosophy.
