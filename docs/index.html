Justifying the State Edited by Gene Callahan & Leye Komolafe

An edited volume in political philosophy

Justifying the State

Is there a duty to obey the state? Fifteen scholars examine the most important modern attempts to justify state authority, from Bosanquet, Gentile, Schmitt and Heidegger to Oakeshott, Rawls, Habermas, Raz, Gaus and Finnis, along with challenges to the very question.
